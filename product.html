    Size guide

    CEEO size chart. Need a different fit? We alter on your measurements.

    How to measure

    1. Bust — around the fullest part of your chest, tape level.
    2. Waist — around your natural waist, the narrowest point.
    3. Hip — around the fullest part of your hips.
    4. Length — from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.

    Between sizes? Choose the larger size for a relaxed fit.
